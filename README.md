# 🧘‍♀️ Aarogya – AI Powered Fitness App

> **An AI-driven fitness coach that empowers everyone to train smarter, eat better, and stay motivated — anytime, anywhere.**

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Most fitness enthusiasts struggle to maintain a consistent routine due to:
- Lack of personal trainers  
- Improper posture and form during workouts  
- Unbalanced nutrition  
- High costs of personalized coaching  

**Aarogya** solves these problems by offering **AI-powered real-time posture correction**, **personalized workouts**, and **nutrition insights**, making fitness **accessible, affordable, and injury-free** for everyone.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Aarogya is your **personal AI fitness companion**, combining computer vision, machine learning, and personalized health analytics to deliver a complete wellness experience.

### ✨ Key Features
- **🤖 Real-Time Pose Correction**  
  - Uses TensorFlow Lite, OpenCV, and MediaPipe for AI-based pose detection.  
  - Instantly analyzes and corrects body posture during workouts.  

- **🥗 AI Nutrition Guide**  
  - Provides smart meal analysis and macronutrient recommendations.  
  - Personalized diet planning for balanced daily nutrition.  

- **🔥 Motivational Challenges**  
  - Engage in community fitness challenges and leaderboards.  
  - Stay consistent through gamified motivation and rewards.  

- **💪 Wearable Integration**  
  - Compatible with Fitbit, Wear OS, and Bluetooth fitness trackers.  
  - Tracks steps, heart rate, and calories burned.  

- **🔐 Secure & Scalable Architecture**  
  - Built with end-to-end encryption and anonymized user data.  
  - Cloud-based backend ensures smooth scalability.

---

## ⚙️ Technical Approach

1. **Pose Estimation Model**:  
   Trained using **MoveNet** for real-time motion tracking and form correction.

2. **Personalized Recommendations**:  
   Adaptive AI algorithms analyze user progress and customize workout and nutrition plans.

3. **Mobile-First Development**:  
   Optimized using Jetpack Compose for a modern, fluid UI.

4. **Data Security**:  
   End-to-end encryption with anonymized analytics.

5. **Scalability**:  
   Cloud-based infrastructure for multi-user performance and wearable data streaming.
